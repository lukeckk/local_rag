#  Quota and levy for S Pass 
You are limited by a quota and must pay the monthly foreign worker levy for each S Pass holder you employ. Find out your quota and levy requirements, how to pay the levy and applying for a levy waiver.
  * ## [Levy and quota requirements Levy and quota requirements for employing S Pass holders. ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/quota-and-levy/levy-and-quota-requirements)
  * ## [Paying the S Pass levy Includes paying by GIRO, viewing levy bills online and penalties for late payment. ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/quota-and-levy/paying-the-s-pass-levy)
  * ## [Apply for levy waiver When and how you can apply for a levy waiver for S Pass holders. ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/quota-and-levy/apply-for-levy-waiver)
