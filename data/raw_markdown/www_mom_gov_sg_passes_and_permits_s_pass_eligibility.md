#  Eligibility for S Pass 
Only eligible candidates will be considered for an S Pass. Criteria include earning a salary of at least $3,300, which is benchmarked against the top one-third of the local associate professionals and technicians (APT) salaries by age.
Use the [Self-Assessment Tool](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/eligibility#self-assessment-tool) to check a candidate’s eligibility before you apply.
## Who is eligible
The S Pass is for**foreign employees** across all nationalities or citizenships 
  * Have a job offer in Singapore.
  * Earn a [fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) comparable to the top one-third of the local APT workforce by age, as shown below:

Sector | For renewal of passes expiring from 1 Sep 2025 to 31 Aug 2026 | For new applications submitted from 1 Sep 2025 / For renewals of passes expiring from 1 Sep 2026 | For new applications submitted from 1 Jan 2027, and renewal of passes expiring from 1 Jan 2028  
---|---|---|---  
**All

(increases progressively with age from age 23, up to $5,650 at age 45 and above)   
  
With adjustments to the S Pass qualifying salary, some employers may wish to convert some of their S Pass holders to Work Permit holders to retain their experience and skills.
To facilitate this, we recognise the past experience that S Pass converters had accumulated while holding an Employment Pass (EP), S Pass and Work Permit. This will be counted towards the worker’s years of experience when assessing their eligibility for higher-skilled (R1) levy status.
## Qualifications
Declaring qualifications is **optional**.
If you do declare your candidates’ qualification in the work pass application, you are responsible for ensuring that your candidate’s qualification:
  * Is authentic
  * Was awarded by an accredited institution in the application form's drop-down list 


## Self-Assessment Tool
Use the [Self-Assessment Tool (SAT)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-s-pass-self-assessment-tool) to check a candidate's eligibility before you apply.
If the SAT shows that the candidate is not eligible, you shouldn’t apply for the S Pass because it will be rejected.
If the SAT shows that the candidate is eligible, there is around a 90% chance that the candidate will get a pass.
## Passes for family members
Pass holders who have a fixed monthly salary of at least $6,000 are eligible for a [Dependant’s Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass) for their spouse and children.
Employers can submit the application together with the S Pass application or separately at a later date.
