#  Renew an Employment Pass 
You can apply to renew an Employment Pass (EP) if you want the pass holder to continue working for you. If the EP is expiring from 1 September 2024, they will need to pass the [COMPASS requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/renew-a-pass#renewal-criteria).
## At a glance
When to renew  |  You can apply to renew an EP **up to 6 months** before the pass expires. You must apply before the expiry date. [Check when you can start renewing](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/renew-a-pass#when-you-can-renew). For EP (Sponsorship): You can start applying to renew **3 months** before the pass expires.  
---|---  
Who can renew  |  Employer or appointed employment agent (EA).   
Duration of renewed pass  |  Up to 3 years.   
How much it costs  | 
  * $225 to get the pass issued
  * $30 for each [Multiple Journey visa](https://www.ica.gov.sg/#/visitor/visitor_entryvisa) (if applicable)

  
How long it takes  |  Renew using [_myMOM_ Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/mymom-portal): Processed or given an update within 10 business days. For EP (Sponsorship) cases only: Within 8 weeks for most cases.   
## Renew early
  * Remember to apply for renewal early. Renewing early does not mean that you'll lose the remaining days in your current pass. The duration of the renewed pass will start when the current pass expires.
  * If you miss the deadline, you need to apply for a new pass.
  * You’ll have to apply for renewal using the [renewal form](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/ep_sponsor_renewal_form.pdf) for EP (Sponsorship) holders.


## Renewal criteria
The candidate needs to:
  * Earn at least the [EP qualifying salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#ep-qualifying-salary). 
  * Work for the same employer in a managerial, executive, or specialised job.
  * Pass the points-based [Complementarity Assessment Framework (COMPASS)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#compass).


  * Candidates earning at least $22,500 [fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) are [exempted from COMPASS](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#exemptions-from-compass).
  * Renewals are not guaranteed. 
  * Once a candidate’s [qualification has been verified](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/documents-required#changes-to-verification-requirement), there is no need to get it verified again at renewal, or when there is a change of employer. If a candidate’s qualifications need to be verified, you will be prompted when filling in the EP application form.


## How to renew
Log in to [_myMOM_ Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/mymom-portal) to apply for renewal.
Ensure that your organisation’s [turnover information](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes#change-in-companys-name-or-financial-information) for the past 3 years has been updated before submitting the application.
### For EP (Sponsorship)
To renew EP (Sponsorship):
  1. Download the [EP (Sponsorship) renewal application form](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/ep_sponsor_renewal_form.pdf) and complete it as a soft copy.
  2. Submit the completed EP (Sponsorship) renewal application form and the related supporting documents using the [online form](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/\\\\go.gov.sg\\renew-ep-sponsorship).


The outcome will be emailed to the contact person indicated in the application.
## When you can renew
You can apply to renew 6 months (180 days) before the pass expires.
Enter the pass expiry date to check when you can start renewing.

## Upon renewal
If your renewal is successful, you:
  1. Will get an in-principle approval (IPA) letter. It is valid for **3 months**.
  2. Must follow the instructions in the IPA to [get the pass issued](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#get-the-pass-issued).


You must get the pass issued before the IPA or current pass expires, whichever occurs first. Otherwise, an overstaying fine will be imposed. The duration of the renewed pass will start when the current pass expires.
## After the pass is issued
After the pass is issued, **check the notification letter** to see:
If the notification letter states | The existing card  
---|---  
Continue using the existing card. |  Must be kept by the pass holder. Note: If you return it when you don’t need to, you have to pay $65.40 (including GST) to [replace the pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/replace-a-pass-card).  
[Getting a new card](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#receive-the-card). | Must be cut in half and discarded to prevent misuse.   
  
  
Inform your pass holder to download and set up their [SGWorkPass ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/sgworkpass)app if they have yet to do so. They will need a Singpass account. They can view their digital work pass and employment information in the app.
